# Bundled photographic sky source

Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky), 4K HDRI.
Original: Greg Zaal. Sky edits: Jarod Guest.
Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky
License: CC0. https://polyhaven.com/license

The imported TextureCube is bundled for this exercise. Sky rendering, exposure and lighting are authored in the course project. The required Fab meshes, materials and textures are also included in the private Week 11 repository and starter ZIP. Students do not need a Fab download or asset migration. See the repository SOURCES.md for attribution and distribution limits.
