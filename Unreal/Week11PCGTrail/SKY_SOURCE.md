# Bundled photographic sky source

Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky), 4K HDRI.
Original: Greg Zaal. Sky edits: Jarod Guest.
Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky
License: CC0. https://polyhaven.com/license

The imported TextureCube is bundled for this exercise. Sky rendering, exposure and lighting are authored in the course project. Other Fab meshes and textures are acquired separately by each student and are not redistributed in the starter ZIP.
