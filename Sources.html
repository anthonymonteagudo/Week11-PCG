Sources and reuse

Original class material: PCG graphs, graph instances, museum demonstrations, trail terrain and lesson explanations prepared for Prof. Anthony Monteagudo's Technical Art course. Learning references inform the concepts; paid tutorial projects, graphs, transcripts and downloaded course archives are not included.

  • Old West Learning, Dekogon / Epic Games, Fab listing. Required meshes, materials and texture dependencies retain their original /Game/Northwood package paths and applicable source license. Student-copy texture resolution is reduced for distribution.
  • Forest/snow vegetation dependencies: existing instructor TechArtGym environment assets and Week 9 surface textures retain their source ownership and licenses. Inclusion in a private class project does not assign a new open license to these assets.
  • Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky), Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest, Poly Haven, CC0. A photographic sky, not dynamic weather.
  • Ben Cloward, PCG video series starting point: point data, masks, spline boundaries, fences and overlap reasoning.
  • Brandon Vox, instructor-provided Udemy PCG notes: graph organization, subgraphs, attributes and modular building concepts. Our native project targets UE 5.7.4; course downloads are not dependencies.
  • Anastasia Opara, Lake Houses: relationships, relative placement and connected assembly. Attributed lecture reference images are distinct from our original building example.
  • Epic Games, PCG documentation.
  • SideFX, Houdini Engine for Unreal: optional instructor showcase. No Houdini software or license is needed to open the main student project.

The included lecture reader has full references and visual credits, including the named GDC examples, procedural methods book, game-design articles, SideFX examples and open-source media notices. The native demo gallery contains actual class captures rather than generated Unreal interfaces.

This repository and the full student ZIP are private teaching distributions. Third-party assets are not relicensed under MIT or a blanket course license. Do not extract and publicly redistribute source assets or tutorial reference images. The private repository owner controls access.
